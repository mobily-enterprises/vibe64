# Your name and the light or dark appearance

Open **Account settings** with the person-and-gear button at the right of the
app header, then choose the **You** tab. The dialog is the same in Working and
Learning.

On a phone (or a narrow window) **Account settings** fills the screen. Its
three tabs, **You**, **AI Accounts** and **GitHub**, share the width with each
icon above its label, so all of them show without scrolling sideways; a small
amber dot on a tab means that connection needs attention. Close the dialog with
the **X** at its top right. On a wider screen it opens as a centred dialog.

## Your name

Under **Your profile** (You — all projects), type a name in **What should
Vibe64 call you?** and choose **Save name**, or press Enter. Vibe64 uses it in
welcomes and collaboration cues across this installation. Leave the field
blank and save to use your computer account name instead. A name is at most 80
characters; a longer one shows "Name cannot exceed 80 characters." and **Save
name** stays disabled.

## Appearance

Under **Appearance** (This browser), choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.
The whole app switches immediately, including the Learning environment (**L**),
its lesson reader and the Learn page's Lesson tab. **System** follows your device's light or dark setting
and changes when the device does. Light is the default.

The choice is saved in this browser only. Another browser, another device or a
private window starts in Light until you choose again; clearing site data also
resets it. It does not change the appearance of the app shown inside
**Preview**, which belongs to the project.

Colleague can explain these steps but cannot change your name or appearance for
you: they are your own choices in the dialog.
