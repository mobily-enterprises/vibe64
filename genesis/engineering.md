# Engineering approach

## Profile

- `focused.v1`

## Project requirements

- New status and recovery notices use the shared theme's paired surface and
  foreground tokens, or a semantic warning/error alert. Do not introduce
  component specific grey backgrounds or independent low-contrast text colours.
  Keep disabled notice actions readable in light and dark themes; reuse the
  application-level notice control rule rather than overriding each button
  separately.
